Chess with a wise coach at your side. He watches every move, speaks up before a mistake costs you, and remembers what you need to work on.

A COACH WHO TALKS TO YOU
The Vizier coaches teens and adults; Hopper, a cheerful little knight, coaches kids. They talk like real coaches, out loud if you like, and they don't repeat the same lines game after game. Your clock pauses whenever the coach is speaking.

TAKE IT BACK
About to blunder? The coach pauses the game and explains what's wrong: a piece left hanging, a fork you walked into, a checkmate you missed. Then you choose: take it back, show me, or play on.

HE REMEMBERS
"Remember last game? You left your knight unguarded then, too." The coach keeps notes on every game and brings up your habits when they matter.

THREE THINGS TO REMEMBER
After every game you get three short takeaways: the costly moment, something you did well, and what to work on next.

THE ADVISOR'S REPORT
Every ten games: your blind spots with real numbers, your playing style, how your rating is moving, and three things to focus on, with practice positions taken from your own games.

AN OPPONENT AT YOUR LEVEL
Play a cast of characters who play like real people at your strength: mostly sensible, sometimes sloppy. Coached games stay winnable, but you still have to earn the win.

TEST YOURSELF
When you're ready, play without help. Test games count fully toward your rating. Time controls: no clock, 10 minutes, 15+10 and 30 minutes. No bullet chess.

LEARN AND PRACTISE
