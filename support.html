Common questions

How do I restore my King's Counsel Pro purchase?

Open the app, go to the Me tab, tap "See Pro", then "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then King's Counsel Pro, and choose Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the current billing period.

The coach isn't talking / I can't hear the voice

Check that your device isn't on silent and the volume is up. In the app's settings you can pick the coach voice and turn spoken coaching on or off.

Can my child use the typed questions feature?

Typed questions to the coach are only available for players aged 13 and older. Kids play with Hopper's fully offline coaching, which needs no internet and has no chat.

How do I turn off ads?

King's Counsel Pro (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime) removes all ads. Tap "Go Pro" on the Home tab or "See Pro" in the Me tab. Ads never appear during a game. Players under 13 always get child-directed, non-personalized ads.

The app says I'm offline

The chess engine and coach run on your device and work fully offline. Typed questions to the coach (13+) need an internet connection.
