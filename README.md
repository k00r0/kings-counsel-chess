# King's Counsel Chess

Chess with a live coach at your side, for kids and adults. iPhone, iPad, Android and web.

- **The Vizier** (teens/adults) and **Hopper the little knight** (kids) speak during the game, in a human voice that never repeats itself.
- Before a mistake costs you, the coach pauses the clock: **Take it back · Show me · Play on**.
- He remembers: "Last game you left your knight unguarded too." Three things to remember after each game, an advisor's report every 10 games, drills built from your own mistakes.
- A human-like opponent matched to your level: always winnable, never handed to you.
- Squire, Knight and Master paths with engine-checked lessons and ~900 puzzles.
- Runs offline (Stockfish 18 in WebAssembly). Optional AI helper on Cloudflare for typed questions.

See **GUIDE.md** to set up, build and publish.
