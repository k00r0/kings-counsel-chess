Privacy policy

King's Counsel Chess · last updated October 2026

We built King's Counsel Chess to keep your data on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and we don't run a database of players.

What stays on your device

Your games, rating, lesson progress, settings, the first name you choose (optional) and the coach's notes about your play are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them any time under Me → Reset progress.

Typed questions to the coach (ages 13+)

If you type a question to the coach, the app sends your question, the current chess position and simple game statistics (for example your approximate rating and which kinds of mistakes you make most) to our server on Cloudflare, which passes them to an AI provider (Groq, or Cloudflare Workers AI) to write the answer. We send a random device code so we can limit how many questions are asked; it isn't linked to your identity. We don't store the questions. Don't type personal information into questions. This feature is switched off for players under 13 and can be turned off in Me.

Premium voice (Pro)

For Pro players who turn it on, the coach's sentences are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to be turned into speech. Nothing else is sent.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (never during a game). For players under 18 ads are non-personalized and age-appropriate; for players under 13 ads are child-directed. Adults may be asked for consent where the law requires it, and on iPhone may be asked for permission to track; you can say no and everything still works. Google's handling of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The kids' coach (Hopper) works fully offline, and typed questions are disabled for players under 13.
