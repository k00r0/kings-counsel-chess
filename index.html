King's Counsel Chess

A wise coach sits beside you while you play. He speaks up before a mistake costs you, lets you take it back, explains why, and remembers what you need to work on.

Play in your browser App Store Google Play
A coach that talks

The Vizier for teens and adults, Hopper the little knight for kids. Human-sounding, never repeats himself, and the clock waits while he speaks.

Take it back

Blunder? The coach stops the clock and offers: take it back, show me, or play on. You learn the lesson without losing the game.

He remembers

"Last game you left your knight unguarded, too." After every game: three things to remember. Every ten games: a full advisor's report.

Always winnable

The opponent plays like a human at your level and makes human mistakes. Wins are earned, never handed over.

Three paths

Squire for young kids, Knight up to about 1200, Master towards 1800. Lessons, puzzles, and drills built from your own mistakes.

Test yourself

When you're ready, play without help. Only Test games count fully for your rating. 10, 15|10 and 30-minute games, no bullet.

Help

Does it work offline? Yes. The chess engine and the coach run on your device. Typed questions to the coach and the premium voice use the internet.

What does Pro include? No ads, unlimited take-backs, more typed questions each day, and a premium voice for the coach.
